# Vision

## Problem
Blind students often depend on other people to read, record, or explain study materials for them.

## Goal
Build a free and accessible tool that helps blind and low-vision students study independently.

## Target users
- Blind students
- Low-vision students
- Students who need audio-based study support

## First focus
- English
- Arabic
- Typed text
- Clean PDFs and scanned pages

## Long-term direction
Phronon should become a study assistant that can read, explain, summarize, and support voice-based interaction.
