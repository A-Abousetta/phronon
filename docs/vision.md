# Vision

## Problem
Blind students often depend on other people to read, record, or explain study materials for them.

## Goal
Build a free, local-first, and accessible tool that helps blind and low-vision students study independently.

## Target users
- Blind students
- Low-vision students
- Students who need audio-based study support

## First version focus
- English
- Arabic
- Desktop-first workflow
- Importing study files
- Extracting readable text
- Listening to content with simple playback controls

## Product principles
- Accessibility before convenience
- Keyboard-first interaction
- Local-first architecture
- No paid APIs
- Small and maintainable first release

## Long-term direction
Phronon should become a study assistant that can read, explain, summarize, and support voice-based interaction.
